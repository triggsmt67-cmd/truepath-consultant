# Businesses in ChatGPT research record

Researched November 15, 2026. Client timezone America/Denver. OpenAI announced the beta November 10; terms dated November 9. Existing launch-week coverage means we cannot claim to be first. Early, accurate material for service owners is the opportunity.

## Editorial decision

Create both a commercial page and an educational article. Service intent: eligibility review, owner-approved business facts, and assistance with supported setup. Article intent: decide what to do now, understand the separate checks, and prepare a usable fact sheet. No demand-volume estimate, invented deployment experience, case study, pricing, or ranking promise.

The original contribution is the owner-approved fact-sheet workflow, a labeled fictional appliance-repair description, questions to test an eligible agent, and a reusable CSV. These are our recommendations, not OpenAI ranking factors. Keep this page distinct from the Google Profile service.

## Boundaries and unresolved questions

- True Path’s own eligibility and client eligibility were not tested. No Business Hub claim, identity submission, DNS change, account invitation, feed, or agent was created.
- Availability, review timing, future monetization and feature expansion are not promises. Recheck official docs and the actual account for each engagement.
- Google SEO practices still apply to these new pages: useful distinct purposes, descriptive links, sources, honest examples, indexable public URLs, and no doorway/location variants. This launch is not evidence of search volume or future rankings.
- GA4 remains deferred by the owner. We do not have a working conversion baseline and do not report absent data as zero.
- No large page family, automatic city variants, synthetic reviews, client proof, or claims of OpenAI affiliation were created.

## Research limits

This is a broad launch-week review, not a claim to have found everything on the internet. Official written documentation controls product statements when video commentary conflicts. We did not access private client dashboards, test an actual claim, or observe an agent in production. Dated public guidance is the practical basis for this publication.
