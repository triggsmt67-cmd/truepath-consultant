# Keyword research and page assignment

Reviewed November 15, 2026, after the owner requested stronger title, H1, heading tags and metadata. This is an emerging-topic intent study with Google Trends observations and the site's Search Console baseline. It is not a paid keyword-volume or difficulty report.

## Actual observations

1. **Google Trends, U.S., Web Search, past seven days**, compared as search terms: `ChatGPT business profile`, `ChatGPT Business Hub`, `Businesses in ChatGPT`. Visible average relative indices were **5, 11 and 2**, respectively. These are normalized indices from this comparison, not searches per month, market shares or lead forecasts. The moving seven-day window and sampling can change the numbers.
2. Rising related queries included `chatgpt claim business`, `chatgpt business hub claim`, and `chatgpt business profile`, labeled Breakout. New-product growth from a small base does not establish large absolute demand. Google explains [relative Trends data](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en-GB) and [rising related searches](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4355000?hl=en-GB).
3. **Search Console, this domain, Web, Aug 14–Nov 13, query contains `chatgpt`: 0 reported impressions, 0 clicks, no matching rows.** This is the site's reported baseline, not total market demand. Query filtering/anonymization and lag limit it; spaced `chat gpt` is not matched by this filter. A newly published page has no established performance history.
4. Search-result language repeatedly uses “claim your business,” “add your business to ChatGPT,” “ChatGPT Business Hub,” and “ChatGPT business profile.” These are observed result themes, not volume or difficulty estimates. Official OpenAI and broad walkthroughs already appear; we cannot promise first position or claim to be the first guide.
5. “ChatGPT Business” alone has different intent: OpenAI's paid team workspace. The [official workspace FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8542115-chatgpt-business-general-faq) establishes that distinction. Broad workspace/software queries would attract the wrong visitors for this service.

[Live Trends comparison](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=now%207-d&geo=US&q=ChatGPT%20business%20profile,ChatGPT%20Business%20Hub,Businesses%20in%20ChatGPT). Saved public observation: `google-trends-observation.txt`; site baseline: `search-console-chatgpt-baseline.txt`.

## Keyword map

| Cluster | Search intent | Page assignment | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT business profile setup; ChatGPT business profile help | Hire help preparing/claiming an eligible profile | Service page | Strong business fit; absolute volume and difficulty unknown |

| Businesses in ChatGPT | Understand OpenAI's named offering | Supporting entity in both openings | Official name; lower relative interest in this comparison |
| ChatGPT business profile verification; local-business eligibility | Resolve an implementation question | Guide headings and supported setup scope | Relevant intent; no independent volume measurement |
| ChatGPT Business Agents | Understand separate agent capabilities/eligibility | One supporting guide section | Relevant distinction; not a promise of local agent access |
| ChatGPT Business pricing, team workspace, employee AI subscription | Buy/use the workspace plan | Exclude as targets | Different product and audience |
| Rank #1 in ChatGPT; guaranteed recommendations | Seek ranking promises | Do not target with a guarantee | Unsupported offer; conflicts with documented product limitations |

Do not create a new page for each wording. Service and guide have different jobs and link to one another. Use actual services/location naturally; no town-name variants for this emerging beta.

## Final on-page choices

| Element | Service page | Owner's guide |
|---|---|---|
| Search title | ChatGPT Business Profile Setup \| True Path Digital | ChatGPT Business Hub: How to Claim Your Business |
| H1 | ChatGPT Business Profile setup for service businesses. | How to Claim Your Business in ChatGPT Business Hub |
| Important H2 | Is ChatGPT Business Hub available for your business? | Can a local business use ChatGPT Business Hub? |
| Supporting H2 | You should leave with something you can use. | Claim and verify your ChatGPT business profile |
| Supporting copy | Eligibility review, checked facts, supported claim assistance | Fact sheet, fictional appliance example, owner verification and capability limits |

Other headings stay descriptive of their sections. No need to insert the same phrase into every H2/H3 or to manufacture an H3 where there is no subsection. Preserve the current canonical URLs; changing a slug solely to repeat a keyword is not warranted.

Google may generate a different search title; align the title, prominent H1, copy, social metadata and link text without stuffing. [Google's title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link). The site already has an inherited meta-keywords tag; no new keyword list was added for this project, and [Google does not use that tag](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags). Meta descriptions should accurately summarize each page, not promise a ranking.
