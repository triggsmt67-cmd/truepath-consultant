# Google SEO working standard

Reviewed November 15, 2026, before the Lead Response and Follow-Up service-page revision. This records the current guidance consulted, not a certification or completed page audit. No page content or deployment changed during this review.

## Official sources

- [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): read the current guide; its displayed update date is December 10, 2025. A review today does not make it a newly published policy.
- [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): eligibility, technical requirements, and core practices.
- [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): checked keyword stuffing, doorway abuse, link spam, and scaled content abuse.

## Working checklist

1. Confirm the exact page’s Search Console queries and indexing status. Keep website-search data separate from Maps metrics and business outcomes.
2. Explain the real service with a descriptive title, main heading, and useful opening. Use search language naturally. Add original practical value and verified claims.
3. Connect relevant resources with descriptive links. Use clear, relevant images and accurate alt text. Verify mobile usability, crawl access, canonical URL, and visible content.
4. Avoid keyword stuffing, repetitive city pages, manipulative links, and mass-produced content without value. No arbitrary word count or heading count. Structured data must accurately describe visible content; rich-result eligibility is a separate check.
5. Save the baseline and verify the release. Assess changes after recrawl and enough comparable data; no ranking guarantee.

## Generative AI content review

Reopened [Google’s generative AI content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) November 15, 2026; displayed update date October 1, 2026.

AI can help research and organize original material. Generating many pages without user value can violate scaled-content policies. Manually verify accuracy, usefulness, and trustworthiness before publishing, including titles, descriptions, image alt text, and structured data. Validate markup against the relevant feature’s policies. Give creation context where useful to readers; the page does not impose a universal AI disclosure label on every service page. Merchant Center has separate requirements.

For this project, natural voice is an editorial goal. Removing em dashes or AI-sounding phrases is not a substitute for original value, factual verification, or honest representation of experience.
